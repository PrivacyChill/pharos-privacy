Privacy. Almost nothing is collected.

This site doesn’t use cookies, analytics, trackers, ads or forms, and it doesn’t load anything from other websites.

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub (a US company, part of Microsoft) processes your IP address to deliver the pages and logs it for security, like any web host. GitHub’s privacy statement applies to that.
  • Searching the database happens in your browser. What you type isn’t sent anywhere.
  • If you email me, I’ll only use your address to reply.

An earlier version of this site had a “join the waitlist” form. It never sent or stored anything.

You can ask me what data I hold about you (in practice, only emails you’ve sent me) and ask me to delete it. You can also complain to a data protection authority, such as the ICO in the UK or the authority where you live in the EU.
