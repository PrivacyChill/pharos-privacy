AI Act triage. Three questions, a rough answer.

Answer three questions to see the likely category of an AI system under the EU AI Act, and which articles to read. It’s a starting point, not legal advice.

1. What does the system mainly do?
2. Who uses it, and where?
3. How much does a person check what it does?

What to do next

    Not legal advice. A simplified triage by me, Lorenzo (CIPP/E). A real assessment depends on the intended purpose, how and where the system is used, whether you build it or use it, and the exact wording of Articles 2, 5, 6 and 50 and Annex III of the AI Act. Last checked September 2026.
