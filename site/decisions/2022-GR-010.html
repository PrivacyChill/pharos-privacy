What happened

The Greek DPA fined the National Bank of Greece €20,000 for violating Article 13 GDPR by not informing customers that the chip of their debit/credit cards collected information on the last 10 transactions. The DPA also held that the controller installed the chip without a valid legal basis and in breach of the principle of transparency.
